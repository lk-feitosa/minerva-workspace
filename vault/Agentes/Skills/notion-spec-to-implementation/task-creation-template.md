[What needs to be done]

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] [Criterion 2]

[Technical approach or notes]

## Dependencies
- Blocked by: [Task] or None
- Blocks: [Task] or None

## Resources
- [Link to design]
- [Link to related code]

## Progress
[To be updated during implementation]
